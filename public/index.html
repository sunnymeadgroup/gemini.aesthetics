Questions

Before you book.

Still unsure? Message us on Instagram or WhatsApp and we'll point you to the right appointment.

What should I book if I'm unsure?

Start with a consultation. We'll assess your skin, talk through what you'd like to achieve and guide you to the right option.

Do I need a consultation before treatment?

Yes. All new clients, and anyone trying a new treatment, need a consultation first so we know it's safe and suitable for you.

Who will I see?

Lucy, Senior Nurse Practitioner and founder, carries out injectables and treatment planning. Vic, our Nurse Prescriber, assesses and prescribes anti wrinkle treatments. Lottie, our Skin Specialist, looks after facials and skin treatments.

I want anti wrinkle injections. What do I need to know?

All anti wrinkle treatments have to be assessed and prescribed by a Nurse Prescriber. New clients will have a consultation with Vic before treatment.

What is a Membership full face consultation?

A nurse led consultation for anyone interested in The Gemini Collective. It includes a full facial assessment, a personalised plan and a chat about membership. A £25 deposit applies and comes off your first payment.

Can I book a treatment without a consultation?

Treatment appointments are for existing clients following an agreed plan. If you're not sure, book a consultation.

I still have questions.

Message us on Instagram, WhatsApp or email and we'll help you choose.
